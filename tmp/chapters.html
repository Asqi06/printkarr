BEFORE YOU PRINT

A few things
worth knowing.

Get order help ${icon('arrow-up-right')}
Can I print only selected pages?

Yes. Choose your page range, copies, single or double sides, and B&W, colour or selected colour pages. Preview your document before confirming.

Can I combine prints and stationery?

Yes, from the same shop. Multiple documents and that shop’s available stationery can share a basket. Items from different shops need separate orders.

How much is delivery?

Enter your location and choose an available delivery option. Your order shows the confirmed delivery charge, printing, processing and any extras before payment.

Do I need an account or wallet to start?

Start your upload without signing in. Authentication happens in the order journey. Wallet offers are optional; available payment methods appear before you pay.

Are self-service kiosks available now?

Kiosks are in development. Public kiosk ordering and QR collection are unavailable. View kiosk status.
