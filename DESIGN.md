# PrintKarr Design System Direction

> Designed with Antislop principles: Minimal, startup-app-based, content-driven, zero AI slop.

## Design Read
- **Product:** On-demand document printing & study stationery delivery desk.
- **Audience:** College students (LIT Sarigam, ROFEL Vapi), school parents, local businesses, and exam aspirants.
- **Visual Language:** Startup consumer utility (clean, crisp, honest, high contrast).
- **Dials:** ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Visual Hierarchy & Palette (R-29)
- **Base Ground:** Pure white `#ffffff` and soft neutral `#f9fafb`.
- **Text & Contrast:** Deep slate ink `#111827` (WCAG AAA compliant on white).
- **Secondary Slate:** `#4b5563` (WCAG AA compliant: 7.1:1 contrast).
- **Primary Action & Conversion:** Dark-store evergreen `#0c831f` (Buttons, Add, Free delivery progress).
- **Speed Accent:** Canary yellow `#f8cb46` (Used sparingly for dispatch status and live delivery pills).
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle slate `#e5e7eb` (1px clean structural edges, no floating blurry cards).

## Anti-Slop Enforcement
- **No Em Dashes (R-02):** Use commas, colons, parentheses, or periods.
- **No Fake Stats or Buzzwords (R-16, R-17):** Real rates (₹1.50/page B&W, ₹4.00/page Colour, ₹149 free delivery).
- **No Monotonous Bento Grids or 3-Step Templates (R-05):** Purpose-built sections for ordering, bundles, catalogue, and tracking.
- **No Generic Pill Shapes Everywhere (R-11):** Radius-12px and 16px for cards, distinct interactive buttons, clear input boundaries.
- **Every Interactive Element Works (R-26, R-35):** 100% functional drawers, steppers, cart, and tracking.
