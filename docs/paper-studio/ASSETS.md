Art direction prompt: Create a bespoke flat 2D risograph editorial illustration for an adult student printing service. Combine a cobalt-blue desktop printer, sheets with abstract blue printed marks, a coral notebook, lime pencil, a small coral flower and a few paper shapes. Use paper grain and strong graphic silhouettes; palette cobalt #234be8, ink #171d26, warm white, lime #dcef87 and coral #ffab95. Transparent wide 3:2 composition, no lettering, UI, gradients, photorealism, CGI or 3D render. Keep it joyful, grown-up and appropriate for an independent print studio.

Screenshots in this directory document desktop and mobile home, upload, stationery, shops, customer account, wallet, contact, pricing and 404 screens. Preview account data and quoted rates are fixture data, not live account balances.
